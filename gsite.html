The Game

There are things a person tries to forget. Fears hidden deep inside, weaknesses they refuse to accept, and thoughts they avoid confronting.

In this psychological horror game, you will experience the difficult journey of a man who has learned to put himself above everything else. A man who has become used to avoiding the things that challenge him and continuing through life without ever truly looking within himself.

But eventually, every person must face the things they tried to leave behind.

As the reality around him begins to change, he will find himself trapped in a world where his fears take form. To move forward, he must confront not only what stands before him, but also what he has kept hidden inside for years.

Explore dark environments, face fears that have taken physical form, and discover a story about human nature, selfishness, and the difficult process of coming face to face with yourself.

Sometimes, the hardest opponent is not the one standing in front of you.
It is the one you carry inside.
